My name is Dr Richard Matthews. I’m a digital forensic scientist and founder of RHEM Labs in South Australia. My work sits at the intersection of media forensics, scientific validation, and courtroom‑ready evidence — especially where modern tooling (AI, platforms, compression, sensors) turns “what happened” into a technical question.

In plain terms: I help answer whether digital material is reliable, how it was produced or altered, and what can (and cannot) be concluded from it — with methods that are documented, testable, and explainable.

Public-facing pillars

  • Digital Truth — establishing whether digital material is authentic, manipulated, or misleading, by examining pixels, audio, metadata, compression artefacts, and system behaviour.
  • From Code to Consequence — tracing how software, models, and digital workflows translate into real‑world effects, including physical devices, manufacturing processes, safety risks, and downstream harm.
  • Open Source, Open Justice — using open‑source intelligence, public data, and reproducible methods to verify claims in a way that can be scrutinised, repeated, and challenged.
  • Evidence on Trial — applying forensic methods that survive admissibility tests, cross‑examination, and judicial scrutiny, with an emphasis on validation, limitations, and error rates.
  • Power, Policy & Narrative — analysing how institutions, regulators, and media respond to evidence, including the incentives, pressures, and narratives that shape decision‑making.

Selected work

  • Curriculum Vitae — professional history, publications, and appointments.
  • Sensor Pattern Noise (SPN) and provenance research — practical methods for camera/media attribution and validation.
  • Forensic workflow design — standards‑aware pipelines intended to be defensible and reproducible.
  • Open artefacts and writeups via this site and linked repositories (where publication is appropriate).

I’m available for contract as a forensic consultant and expert witness where my expertise is relevant. Engagements are scoped to the question, the evidentiary constraints, and the required standard of proof.

As always, feel free to contact me to collaborate.
